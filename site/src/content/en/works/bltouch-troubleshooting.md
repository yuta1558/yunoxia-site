## Take it apart

When a BLTouch probe pin won't deploy under Klipper, or triggers falsely, the information is scattered across forum threads. There are several possible causes, and treating only the symptom tends to send you the long way round.

## Understand it

The causes fall roughly into three groups: the pull-up setting on the signal line, the filter capacitor for noise, and the wiring itself. I organised them so you can narrow down which one to suspect, step by step, from the symptom.
